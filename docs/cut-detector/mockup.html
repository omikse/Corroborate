Cut detector — mockup

An edited video, cut in five places and shuffled. Every cue lane is drawn under the picture and the sound; a red line is a cut, and each piece leaves as a recording of its own. Click a cut to switch it off or on, click empty space to add a cut by hand, drag a lane's ◂ (right end) to move its threshold.

Events confirm, they don't cut

Any node's event output can be wired in: single moments with an error. One near a cut shows as a blue "+" chip in the table. The event at 0:33.0 has no cue beside it, so it stays a mark; click there to cut by hand.
