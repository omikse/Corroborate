  1. One fixed grid for every row (name, sign, fields, error margin, remove), so the header, the rows, + row and Result line up.
  2. Each time reads as two groups: the date (year-mo-day) and the clock (h:min:s.ms), with the separators between the boxes.
  3. Square, compact boxes, 22 px high.
  4. Fields that hold 0 are dimmed, so the real offset stands out; nothing is hidden.
  5. The input fed from a socket: a blue wire into a blue frame around its fields, the fields grey and locked.
  6. A row fed from a socket: a red wire into a red frame around its fields, its fields and ± grey and locked.
  7. A row typed by hand: no wire, no frame, its fields active.
  8. Wires drop just under the row's label and pass under the sign button into the frame; no text cuts them.
  9. Every row has its sign button, wired or not; a click turns it over.
  10. The error margin column is named "error margin ±" and sits apart from the fields, with a wider gap.
  11. One root sign spans the error margin column, with ² on each box; hovering it shows the sum, as the node does.
  12. + row is a single grey strip, as wide as the fields.
  13. An empty dashed socket waits level with + row; wiring a time into it adds the next row.
  14. Result sits in one blue frame around its fields and its ± box, read-only, since the output carries both; the output line leaves that frame, runs under the "time" label, and reaches the socket.

Style study: salvaged machine

The same node in a rag-doll-and-rust look: burlap body, a rusty riveted title plate, paper boxes with ink numbers, brass buttons for sockets and signs, stitched thread for wires and frames, a lamp on the output, all with a slight hand-drawn wobble. Every texture is drawn in code (noise and gradients), no images. The numbers stay monospace and sharp.

Style study 2: brass odometer

From the painted reference: a riveted rusty plate, a brass title strip, brass name plates, every box an odometer drum in a dark window, brass ring sockets with twisted cables, and Result lit behind a brass frame. Kept from the real node, where the painting drifted: every row keeps its socket at its own height, the ± column stays one column with its root sign, and Result shows the time as well as the ±.
